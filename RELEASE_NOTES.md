# Starboard 12.7

The first public release of Starboard, an all-in-one companion app for Star Citizen.

## What it does

- **Where am I:** press F9 in game and Starboard shows your exact position, what is nearby, and guides you to any place, cave or saved spot.
- **Mining:** identify scanned rocks (F8), find where any ore is, get into the Aaron Halo and other belts with the exact quantum drop-out point, track refinery jobs, and plan your mining loadout.
- **Hauling and trade:** the best trade routes for your ship, hauling contracts, and one-trip planning that combines contracts, refinery pickups and a trade.
- **Salvage and combat:** where the wrecks are, where to sell salvage, bounty payouts, and where security and outlaws are.
- **Missions and crafting:** every mission with its reward and the blueprints it gives, and crafting plans with the materials you need.
- **Shop and hangar:** where to buy any item or ship, your own ships, and a full ship loadout builder with stealth and signature details.
- **Money:** your balance and every payout, filled in from the game's own log as you play.
- **In-game overlay, alerts, tickers and events:** your route and timers in game, alerts for refinery jobs and server status, market and news tickers, and a calendar of major events.

## Install

1. Download **StarboardSetup-12.7.exe** below and run it. No Python needed, and no admin prompt.
2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Starboard is not code-signed (signing costs money and Starboard is free), so Windows warns until enough people have run it.
3. Starboard opens with a short welcome guide.

Your data is kept in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Starboard`, so new versions and reinstalls never touch it.
